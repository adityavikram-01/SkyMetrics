# SkyMetrics map geometry

India state and union-territory boundaries in `india-states.svg` are derived from
[geoBoundaries gbOpen India ADM1](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/),
by William & Mary geoLab and contributors. Source geometry was simplified and
projected for this interactive route map. License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Airport markers use approximate airport coordinates. Flight arcs show routes in
the SkyMetrics demo dataset; the map does not imply coverage of every domestic
flight or airport.
